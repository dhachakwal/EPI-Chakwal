Campaign Details

  1. Your Union Council is fixed by your login and is shown below.
  2. On each Area In-charge sheet, add that Area In-charge's team members using the “Add Team Member” box. Every detail fills in automatically; only the signature space stays blank.
  3. Mark attendance on each sheet: click the DAY 1–4 cells to tick them and the Training cell to choose Yes or No, or use the “All Attendance OK” button. The ticks print on the sheet and are saved in the Excel file.
  4. When all team members are assigned, download the Excel file or the PDF, take a printout, and submit it as before. A single Area In-charge sheet can also be downloaded on its own.
