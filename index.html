Official Events & Updates
8th August 2026
Sweep-up Activity for improving EPI coverage has started in District Chakwal. Phase-1 covers all Urban UCs across every tehsil; Phase-2 will cover the remaining UCs. An emergency meeting of Vaccinators and ASVs was held: Tehsil Chakwal vaccinators at CEO Office, DHA Chakwal, while vaccinators of other tehsils met at their respective DDHO Offices under DDHO/ASV supervision, remaining connected with the District via Zoom.

10th August 2026
Field Supervision: The District Health Officer (Preventive Services) and the District Superintendent Vaccination (DSV), Chakwal, conducted field visits during the sweep-up activity. They reviewed teams in the field, visited households, and inquired from parents about the vaccination status of their children. Vaccinators were directed to ensure that no child remains unvaccinated.

Tehsil-wise Summary — Drop-out Rates

District Chakwal | High drop-out: >+10% or <−2% between consecutive doses

Tehsil Avg BCG→P1 Avg P1→P3 Avg MR1→MR2 UCs with High DO (>10%)
Chakwal +4.9% −2.0% +10.5% 36
Choa Saidan Shah −33.4% −11.0% +10.3% 8
Kallar Kahar −40.8% −220.8% +10.2% 9
Talagang −18.4% −33.3% +19.7% 29
Key Observations
  • Chakwal is relatively better on BCG→P1 and P1→P3; MR1→MR2 drop-out still high (+10.5%).
  • Kallar Kahar shows extreme P1→P3 anomaly (−220.8%) — likely data quality / reporting issue; needs urgent verification.
  • Choa Saidan Shah & Talagang have high drop-out across BCG→P1 and P1→P3 pathways.
  • Total UCs with high drop-out (>10%): 82 (Chakwal 36 + Talagang 29 + Kallar Kahar 9 + Choa 8).
  • Action: Investigate defaulters, session quality, and data anomalies in high-DO UCs.
Note: High drop-out is defined as >+10% or <−2% between consecutive doses. Negative values often indicate catch-up vaccination or data inconsistency.
